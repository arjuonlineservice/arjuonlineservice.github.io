ABOUT US

Empowering Rural India Through Digital Services

ARJU ONLINE SERVICES is a government-verified Common Service Centre (CSC) and the head office of Airtel Payments Bank in the entire Barpeta district of Assam.

Located in the heart of Kalgachia at Haldia Pather, Ghuigubari, we have been serving thousands of customers with fast, reliable and affordable digital services. Our mission is to bridge the digital divide and bring every government and banking service to the doorsteps of rural Assam.

From PAN cards to LPG booking, from Micro ATM to bill payments — we handle everything with care, speed and transparency. Our trained staff ensures that every customer gets the best experience.
